How to play

A battle takes a few minutes. The rules take one.

Flip two cards

Each turn brings a new target number. Flip any two face-down cards.

Make the target

Pick + − × or ÷. Get it right to attack and keep flipping. Combo hits grow stronger.

Remember everything

Cards stay in place after flipping back. Can't make it? Tap PASS. A wrong answer backfires.
