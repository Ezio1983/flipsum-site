Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the FlipSum app (“the app”) uses and why. In short: you don't need an account, we don't run analytics, and we don't sell your data.

Information we collect

We don't ask for your name, email or any account. We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we have no server that receives your gameplay data.

Game progress

Your settings, records and unlocks are saved on your device. If iCloud is enabled, some progress may also sync through your own iCloud account, which is handled by Apple. We cannot access it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device type, operating system, and how you interact with ads.

The app does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so Google cannot access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Ads are filtered to general-audience content. Buying the Full Version or Remove Ads turns off full-screen ads.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad Privacy Choices.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether you own a purchase.

Children

FlipSum is suitable for all ages. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
