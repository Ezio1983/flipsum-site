FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

I bought Remove Ads in the old version. Do I keep it?

Yes. On iOS 18 or later, the new version detects your previous Remove Ads purchase and turns off ads automatically. If ads still appear, tap Restore Purchases in Settings, or email us.

How do I unlock Stormhawk without paying?

Defeat Stormhawk in Solo Challenge to earn Wind Feathers. Collect them all and Stormhawk is yours for good. You can also try it for one battle by watching an optional ad.

What happens if I answer wrong?

A wrong answer costs you 2 HP. If the two cards can't make the target, tap PASS instead. It's free.

Can I replay the tutorial?

Yes. Go to Settings and tap Replay Tutorial.

How do I change the language?

FlipSum follows your device language. On iPhone or iPad, go to Settings › Apps › FlipSum › Language to choose English, Japanese or Traditional Chinese.
